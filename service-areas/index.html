What each town page covers

Each page lists the ZIP codes we serve there, a note on local water or flooding drawn from public reports, a Before We Arrive checklist, and the services that town calls about most. We do not serve Indiana, so Hammond and the rest of Lake County are outside our area even though Calumet City borders them.

Why the south suburbs share so many plumbing problems

These towns share the same weather. The Cook County hazard mitigation plan for Dolton puts average precipitation near 36 inches a year and describes summer rain that often arrives as short, hard storms, with nighttime thunderstorms common. A basement that stayed dry in a slow rain can still take on water when the sewer backs up in a downpour, so most of our flood-season calls start with a floor drain or a sump pump.

The Thornton Composite Reservoir, run by the Metropolitan Water Reclamation District, began filling in November 2015 and serves Dolton, Harvey, Riverdale, South Holland and Calumet City among 14 communities. It captures combined sewer overflow. It does not stop a private basement from flooding through a floor drain or a failed sump, so a working pump and a clear house drain still matter.

How to pick the right page

If you live in Dolton, our sewer line repair and leak detection pages carry the most local detail. If you live in a neighboring town, its page adds that town's water report or flood history, the ZIP codes we serve there, and a Before We Arrive checklist you can use while you wait for the plumber.

Whichever page you use, the next step is the same. Call (773) 207-0518, say what you see, and give the street address. The dispatcher confirms the area, books the first available appointment, and tells you what to do in the meantime.

What we need to know about your address

Tell the dispatcher about any problem that is spreading, such as water through a ceiling or sewage coming up a floor drain, because that changes how we schedule the visit. Mention whether the water is off, and whether anyone can meet the plumber at the door.

A street address lets us confirm the town and ZIP code before we promise a visit. Nearby ZIP codes such as 60428 in Markham, 60429 in Hazel Crest and 60476 in Thornton are covered through the closest town page, so mention yours when you call. For rentals, add the unit number and a contact who can open the door.

Licensing is the same in every town we serve

Illinois licenses plumbers statewide, and the Illinois Plumbing Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code 890) is the standard the work follows in all five towns. Permit rules vary by village and city, so ask your own building office before a larger job. You can confirm any plumber's state license with the IDPH License Verification tool before work starts.
