Water heater replacement in Dolton, IL is the removal of a failed tank and the connection of a new one to water, fuel, venting and a safe drain. Our 24/7 emergency line takes these calls at any hour, because a rusted tank can dump 40 or 50 gallons on a basement floor in the time it takes to find a flashlight.

While you wait, close the cold supply valve and cut the power or gas to the unit. The steps right below walk you through it. Then call the Dolton dispatch line and we will start planning the swap.

Failed tank in the basement: stop the water while you wait

You can do all of this without tools, and it limits the damage to what is already wet.

Shut the cold supply

Turn the valve on the cold pipe above the tank until it stops. If it is stuck, find the main shutoff where the water enters the house and close that.

Turn off power or gas

Electric: flip the breaker for the heater. Gas: turn the control knob to OFF. If there is a gas smell, leave and call Nicor Gas at 888-642-6748 or 911 first.

Do not drain a hot tank

If you open the drain valve to help, scalding water exits the hose. Wait until it cools, or let us handle it.

Keep power away from the water

Do not wade through standing water to reach a panel or outlet. Call from outside the wet area.

Photograph the label and the damage

The label shows size, fuel and age. Photos help with an insurance claim and help us plan the right unit.

Most swaps are done in one visit when the new unit and parts are on the truck.

Sizing a new tank for your house

Tank size is chosen by how many people use hot water at the same hour, not by how big the old one was. A 40 gallon tank can serve a couple. A family of four with two bathrooms often wants 50 gallons, and a larger household may be better off with a bigger tank or a different type.

The first-hour rating on the yellow EnergyGuide label matters more than the raw gallon number, because it says how much hot water the unit can supply in one busy hour. If you are weighing a wall-hung on-demand unit, see tankless water heater for how it differs.

Venting and the expansion tank

Gas tanks must vent combustion gases outdoors, and the old vent pipe may need to be re-sized, re-pitched or replaced. A rusted vent connector is a reason to change it on swap day. We check the flue draft after lighting, because a back-drafting heater can put carbon monoxide into the living space.

If your house has a backflow device or a closed water meter setup, thermal expansion can overpressure the tank, which is why the old relief valve may have leaked. In that case an expansion tank on the cold line is part of the fix. We measure house pressure and tell you the number.
