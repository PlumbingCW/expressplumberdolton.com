A tankless water heater in Dolton, IL heats water only while a tap is open, with no storage tank. We install them, repair them when error codes or cold water appear, and flush the scale out of the heat exchanger. Our line is answered 24/7, and visits are same-day when available.

Tankless units are simple to use and demanding to install. The big differences from a tank heater are gas pipe size, a stainless or PVC vent, and a heat exchanger that scales up when minerals are present.

How a descale flush works

Scale is the main reason tankless units lose output, trip error codes or shut down. A flush dissolves it from inside the heat exchanger. Here is what a pro does.

1

1. Shut off power and fuel

We turn off the gas supply and the unit's power, then close the hot and cold isolation valves on the service valve set.

2

2. Connect a pump and hoses

A small submersible pump and two hoses attach to the service valve ports and form a closed loop through a bucket.

3

3. Circulate vinegar or descaler

We pour in food-grade white vinegar or a descaler made for tankless units and run it through the exchanger for roughly three quarters of an hour. Longer for heavy scale.

4

4. Rinse and clean the filter

We flush with clean water, remove and rinse the cold water inlet filter screen, reopen the valves, purge air and test for stable temperature and no error code.

Why flushing frequency depends on your water

The Village of Dolton buys Lake Michigan water from Chicago, and its water quality report does not list hardness. Because we cannot tell you the figure, we ask you to test your tap with a basic hardness kit, or ask the village for its annual water quality report.

Harder water means faster scale, and a heater with no isolation valves cannot be flushed easily. If your unit has none, we can add them. Skipping flushes with hard water is the most common reason these units lose output within a few years.

Common tankless problems

Most complaints fall into a handful of groups. Error codes differ by brand, so the code on the screen helps.

Cold water sandwich

A burst of hot, then cold, then hot again, often from short run times or a scaled heat exchanger.

Error code and shutdown

Flame failure, exhaust blockage, ignition and flow errors all have different fixes. Look up the code first, then call.

Low flow or weak output

A clogged inlet filter screen or a scaled exchanger cuts flow. Both are fixed with a flush.

Leak at a fitting

Water at the bottom of the unit can be a loose connection or a failed exchanger. We trace which.

No ignition

Dirty sensors, low gas pressure from an undersized line, or a blocked vent can stop firing.

Gas line sizing and venting

Many tankless units draw more gas at full fire than a 40 gallon tank does, so a line that fed the old tank heater may be too small. The manufacturer publishes the BTU demand, and we compare it to pipe size and length. If the gas pipe needs upsizing, the quote shows that work.

If you smell gas at any time, leave the building and call Nicor Gas at 888-642-6748 or 911 before calling us. We do not work around a gas smell.

Tankless or tank: the honest trade-offs

Tankless is not always the better choice. It fits some homes better than others.

Tankless strengths

  • Endless hot water within the unit's flow limit
  • Compact, wall-mounted, saves floor space
  • No standing tank to rupture and flood
  • Often longer service life with regular flushing

Tankless limits

  • Higher purchase and install cost
  • May need a larger gas line and new venting
  • Two showers plus a washer can exceed one unit's flow
  • Needs flushes, especially if your water is hard

If you decide a tank suits you better, see water heater installation. If a tank has already failed, water heater replacement is the faster path.

Standards that govern a tankless water heater

Tankless units draw a large gas flow and need approved venting, so the manufacturer's instructions and the National Fuel Gas Code (NFPA 54) as adopted locally set the sizing and clearances. The Illinois Plumbing Code, 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890, covers the water connections and relief valve.
