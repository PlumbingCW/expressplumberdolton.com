The river, the old sugar factory site and what it means for your basement

Riverdale is a village of about 10,700 residents, ZIP 60827. The Little Calumet River runs along the south side of the old sugar-factory site near the village's northwest edge, where a beet sugar factory opened in 1905 on the site of an 1871 distillery. The village is also served by the Thornton Composite Reservoir, which captures combined sewer overflow.

The reservoir works on the public system. It does not keep a private basement dry, because a failed sump pump or a floor drain that backs up is a problem inside your property line. What a sump pump does and what it cannot do

A sump pump is a pit, a pump and a discharge pipe that moves groundwater out from under the floor. Many homes rely on one, and many people expect it to do more than it can.

What it handles

  • Groundwater that rises in the pit after rain
  • Water collected by drain tile around the footing
  • A steady flow when the float and check valve work
  • Pumping out the basin before it spills

What it does not handle

  • A blocked main line pushing sewage up a floor drain
  • Water that comes through the foundation wall at a crack
  • Any water at all during a power outage with no battery backup
  • A discharge pipe that is frozen or has the wrong slope

If your pump is old, noisy or does not start when you lift the float, our sump pump repair page explains what a technician checks. If water keeps entering after the pump runs, the answer may be basement waterproofing.

Shut off the main valve if a pipe is the source

Turn it clockwise. If the water is coming up from the floor drain or in through the walls, the valve will not stop it, so move to the next step.

Kill power to wet areas

If water covers outlets or reaches the furnace or panel, do not wade in. Call your electric utility, ComEd at 800-334-7661, and call us.

Clear the area

Move valuables and cardboard up off the floor. Photograph the water and the damage for your insurer before anything is moved.

Stop sending water down the drains

Drain backups that arrive with the storm

A basement floor drain that bubbles during rain can mean the house line is blocked, the sewer is overloaded, or both. We run water inside the house first, then a camera, to see whether the blockage is on your side. A cable pass through a clean line is wasted money, so we confirm before we start.

After the water is out: what to do next

The first hours matter for insurance and for mold. A short list keeps you from forgetting something.

  • Photograph damage and keep an inventory of belongings
  • Call your insurance agent and ask what your policy covers
  • Dry the area with fans and open windows where you can
  • Test the sump pump by pouring a bucket into the pit
  • Ask us about a battery backup for the next outage
