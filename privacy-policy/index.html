What this policy covers

This policy describes how Dolton Express Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our line. We take requests by phone only, and this website has no forms, accounts or checkout.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can schedule service. We use that information to schedule and complete your service and to answer questions about it.

Website data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open. We use it to keep the site working and secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Maps and fonts

This site loads Google Maps and Google Fonts. Those services may collect information under their own policies when a page loads.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@expressplumberdolton.com or calling (773) 207-0518. You can also block cookies or scripts in your browser, and the site will still work.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date on this page when we do. Last updated October 2026.
