Sewer line cleaning in Dolton, IL means clearing the main pipe that carries all of the home's waste out to the village sewer. A clog there stops every fixture at once, so toilets, tubs and floor drains back up together. Our 24/7 emergency line is answered nights and weekends.

If sewage is on the floor, stop running water now and keep everyone away from it. Then call us and say which drains are backing up. That tells us whether the clog is in the main or in a branch.

Main line blocked: what to do while you wait

The cleanup is easier if the backup stops growing before we arrive.

Stop the water

Do not flush, do not run laundry, and pause the dishwasher. Each gallon has nowhere to go.

Find the cleanout

Look for a capped pipe near the foundation or in the basement floor. Do not open it, but tell us where it is.

Move belongings

Lift anything that can soak up sewage, such as boxes and rugs, from the floor.

Do not mix chemicals

Bleach with ammonia-based or acid cleaners can release toxic gas. Do not pour any of it down the drain or use it to clean up a backup.

How we clear a main line

We start at the cleanout, because a cable run through a capped pipe is the cleanest way in. A powered cable with a cutting head opens the line, and then we flush it with water to confirm a full flow. If the cleanout is missing, we may need to pull a toilet to reach the line.

If a cable breaks through but the line backs up again soon, we recommend hydro jetting or a camera look. Fast fixes are fine at midnight, but we say so when the result is temporary.

What blocks a main sewer line

Most main line clogs come from a short list of causes.

Tree roots

Roots enter joints and grow into a mat that catches toilet paper and waste.

Grease from the kitchen

Fat that cools in the branch line travels to the main and hardens.

Wipes and hygiene products

Products labeled flushable do not break up like paper and collect at bends.

Collapsed or offset pipe

A cable cannot fix a broken section. We tell you if the line needs repair.

Sediment and scale

Older iron lines narrow with deposits until they cannot carry the flow.

After the backup: cleanup and sanitation

Clearing the line does not clean the floor. Sewage leaves bacteria on every surface it touched. Porous materials such as carpet, drywall and cardboard that soaked it up often cannot be saved. Wear gloves and boots, ventilate, and avoid mixing chemicals while you disinfect.

We clear the plumbing, not the damage. A restoration company handles drying and removal of ruined materials. Take photos first, since your insurer will want them, and read the note below on coverage.
